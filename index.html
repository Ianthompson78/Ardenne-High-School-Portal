Welcome to Ardenne High School

Ardenne High School is a co-educational secondary school located in St. Andrew, Jamaica. Founded in 1927 by Church of God missionaries Rev. George and Nellie Olson, the school has grown from five students to a thriving school community.

Guided by its motto, Deo Duce Quaere Optima, meaning "With God as Guide, Seek the Best", Ardenne encourages academic excellence, personal development and participation in sports, performing arts and other extracurricular activities.
