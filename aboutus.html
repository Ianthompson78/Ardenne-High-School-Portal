Mission Statement

Ardenne is founded on high ethical principles based on the moral teachings of Christianity. Its motto, ‘Deo Duce Quaere Optima’, translates to ‘With God as Guide, Seek the Best’. Ardenne High School is committed to providing quality education in a Christian environment and to inculcating the principles of Christian tolerance and self-discipline as the foundation for self-worth. This is achieved by offering a wide variety of activities, which serve to prepare students to become responsible and contributing members of society.

Facilities and Activities

Ardenne High School sits on a 12-acre campus in Kingston, and over the years the school has added several facilities to support both academic work and student life. The E. M. Claire Gayle Library is a central part of the campus. The building opened in 1981, and its first floor was fully computerised in 2015 with 46 computers running on an N-Computing system. The library also holds a conference room, and the former music and geography rooms have been converted into e-learning and digital media labs. Sixth formers have access to modern, well-equipped science laboratories designed for CAPE studies. In 2011 the school opened the Winston Roberts/NCB Foundation Science Centre, a $30 million facility built to strengthen practical science instruction.

Clubs and Societies

Every Ardenne student is required to take part in at least one co-curricular activity. The school competes in all the major sporting competitions, including swimming, football, basketball, netball, lawn tennis, badminton, and track and field. Basketball and netball are among the school’s strongest sports, and between 2000 and 2008 the basketball team won fifteen championships. Students also participate in chess, table tennis, cricket, and the Cadet Corps.

Clubs and societies cover a broad range of interests. These include the Inter School Christian Fellowship (ISCF), Drama, Dance, Chess, Key Club, the Debating Society, the Tourism Action Club, Choir, Guides, Interact, the Ardenne Development Club, and the Ardennes Coalition of Environmentalist (ACE). The school also fields teams for Schools Challenge Quiz, the Spelling Bee, and the UWI Maths Olympiad, and students take part in the performing arts and drama festivals.
